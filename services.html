Good to Know

Trial Run & Booking Policy

Clarity and preparation for your big day

Booking Policy

  • 60% advance required for confirmation of the booking (non-refundable).
  • Remaining 40% payable post event before departure.
  • Dates are blocked strictly on a first-come, first-served basis.

Travel expenses are borne by the client both ways and are not included in the package.

Trial Details

  • Trial takes place at my place in Vashi, Navi Mumbai.
  • Charges are ₹3,000, payable immediately — this amount is fully deducted from the package once the booking is confirmed.
  • A second trial prior to the wedding day to finalise the look (optional).

To finalise the package, we'd love to discuss your specific requirements and budget — a call is the best way for us to understand each other's needs and preferences.
