Meet the Artist

Deepiksha Anavadia

Professional Makeup Artist

With a passion for enhancing natural beauty, Deepiksha creates refined, personalised makeup looks designed to make every occasion unforgettable.

From the first consultation to the final touch-up before you walk down the aisle, every look is built around your features, your outfit and the story of your day — never a template.

Based in Vashi, Navi Mumbai, with over 5 years of professional experience across 36+ locations, Deepiksha specialises in timeless, elegant, and personalized bridal looks. Her signature is a flawless, long-lasting, and lightweight finish that allows the bride's natural radiance to shine through effortlessly.

Artistry Principles

The Bridal Philosophy

Every detail tuned to your facial anatomy, comfort, and wedding vision

01

Never a Template

Every bride has distinct bone structure, skin tone, and personal aesthetics. We study facial anatomy and colour harmony so jewellery, outfit tones, and makeup blend in complete synergy.

02

Lightweight & Long-Lasting

Your wedding day involves long ceremonies, high emotions, and bright lighting. Our specialized techniques ensure high-definition coverage that feels breathable and stays flawless from morning to midnight.
